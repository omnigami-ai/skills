# Font sources

Fraunces upright and Nunito are copied from the approved Fernwise app.
Fraunces Italic is from the Google Fonts repository:
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/fraunces/Fraunces-Italic%5BSOFT,WONK,opsz,wght%5D.ttf
Retrieved 2026-10-15. Both Fraunces faces pin SOFT=100 and WONK=0;
opsz and wght remain variable. The adjacent fraunces-ofl.txt and
nunito-ofl.txt license the bundled fonts under SIL OFL 1.1.
